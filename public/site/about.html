WHO WE ARE

Steady strategy.

Snapping Turtle Media started as a Delhi-NCR performance studio and grew into a full-funnel creative agency running work across the Gulf, South Asia and — now — East Asia. Same team, same craft, a bigger map.

THE NAME

Why a turtle?

Turtles have survived every mass extinction event in the planet's history — not through speed, but through adaptation. Hard shell, patient pace, and an ability to thrive in almost any environment, from open ocean to riverbank.

That's the operating principle behind the studio. Platforms change, algorithms shift, entire markets open overnight — the brief doesn't change: build brand foundations solid enough to survive the shift, and move fast enough to capture what's next. It's why a studio built in Delhi-NCR could open a Dubai desk, and why Dubai can now brief Singapore with the same playbook.

HOW WE WORK

Three things we don't compromise on.

Strategy before spend

Every retainer starts with positioning and audience work — media buys follow the plan, not the other way round.

In-house, not outsourced

Design, production, CGI and development sit in the same building — no relay race between vendors and timezones.

Reported, not assumed

HOW A PROJECT RUNS

From brief to launch.

The same four-stage process runs whether the brief is a single campaign or a full market entry.

01 — Discover

Audit & positioning

Market, competitor and audience research, translated into a positioning document before a single asset is made.

02 — Design

Creative & system

Identity, content pillars and production plans — built to flex across every market you're launching into.

03 — Build

Production & development

Video, CGI, web and campaign builds run in parallel inside one studio, not handed between agencies.

04 — Grow

Launch & optimize

Live reporting, iteration cycles and monthly reviews — the studio stays on after launch, not just through it.
